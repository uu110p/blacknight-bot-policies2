Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to BCP (Black Night Pay). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed through our bot on our private Discord server.

1. Information We Process

The bot operates exclusively within a private server environment and processes limited data strictly required for its core functionality:

  • Discord User IDs & Mentions: Used solely to associate in-server banking balances, fines, and roleplay vehicle records with the correct user.
  • Roleplay & Economy Data: In-server currency transactions, fine tracking, and vehicle registrations/insurance details.
  • Simulation Logs: Tracking player violations within the Roleplay simulation.

2. Data Storage & Security

No External Exploitation or Harvesting: We do not collect, store, or share any sensitive personal information externally. All simulated data and logs remain strictly within the secure boundaries of our private server environment. No data is sold, monetized, or exposed to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Data associated with the bot's economy and roleplay systems is retained only as long as necessary to maintain the server's simulation progression, or until requested for removal by server administration.

1. About the Bot

BCP is a custom general banking and fine-management bot. Its features include:

  • Banking & Economy: Managing in-server currency transfers, paying roleplay fines, purchasing server buyable ranks, and assisting other members with their fines.
  • Roleplay Management: Tracking player violations within the simulation, registering custom roleplay vehicles, and handling vehicle insurance.

2. In-Server Currency Guidelines

The currency utilized by the bot is strictly an in-game / in-server virtual currency. It holds no real-world monetary value, cannot be exchanged for real currency, and is used solely for roleplay simulation mechanics, purchasing server ranks, and fine settlements.

3. Acceptable Use

Users must not attempt to exploit, bug, or abuse the bot's commands, economy balance systems, or record logs. Any manipulation of the bot's features will result in administrative action within the server.
